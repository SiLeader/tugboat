CSI Control Plane Completion
============================

Goal
Support Filesystem and Block storage with general CSI providers. Track the remaining work on
the API, resource-model, and controller side.

Current evidence
- `tugboat-resources/proto/core/v1/` defines `PersistentVolume`, `PersistentVolumeClaim`, and
  `StorageClass`, and `tugboat-resources/src/manifests/mod.rs` registers them as API resources.
- `tugboat-resource-store/src/serializer/mod.rs` already serializes `PersistentVolume`,
  `PersistentVolumeClaim`, and `StorageClass`, so they can be stored and watched through
  etcd-backed APIs.
- `tugboat-apiserver/src/endpoints/v1_core/` exposes CRUD/list/watch/status endpoints for
  `PersistentVolume`, `PersistentVolumeClaim`, and `StorageClass`.
- `tugboat-controller-manager/src/main.rs` starts both `PvcProvisionerController` and
  `PersistentVolumeCleanupController`.
- `tugboat-controller-manager/src/pvc_provisioner.rs` dynamically provisions CSI-backed volumes
  with `CreateVolume`, creates managed PVs, and binds them back to PVCs. It also handles PVC
  resize: when `requested_capacity_bytes` exceeds current capacity, it calls
  `ControllerExpandVolume`, propagates `node_expansion_required` to `PersistentVolumeStatus`,
  and sets `resize_pending` on `PersistentVolumeClaimStatus`.
- `tugboat-controller-manager/src/pv_cleanup.rs` finalizes managed PV deletion and calls
  `DeleteVolume` on the CSI driver. Non-CSI PVs are handled gracefully (skip without error).
- `StorageClassSpec` carries `controller_expand_secret_ref`, `controller_publish_secret_ref`,
  `node_expand_secret_ref`, `node_publish_secret_ref`, and `node_stage_secret_ref`, and
  `pvc_provisioner.rs` propagates all of these into the provisioned PV's `CsiPersistentVolumeSource`.
- `CsiPersistentVolumeSource` carries `fs_type` and `volume_attributes` for driver-specific
  filesystem configuration; these are passed through `CreateVolumeRequest.parameters` and
  ultimately appear in `NodeStageVolumeRequest` and `NodePublishVolumeRequest`.

What is still missing
- `StorageClassSpec` has no `controller_create_secret_ref` (provisioner secret). As a result,
  `CreateVolumeRequest.secrets` and `DeleteVolumeRequest.secrets` in `pvc_provisioner.rs` and
  `pv_cleanup.rs` are always sent empty. Drivers that require credentials at provision/deprovision
  time (e.g. Ceph RBD, iSCSI-based drivers) will fail or behave incorrectly.
- `StorageClassSpec` has no `mount_options` field. `MountVolume.mount_flags` in
  `tugboat-csi-operator/src/lib.rs` is always `Vec::new()`. Filesystem drivers that require
  non-default mount flags (e.g. `noatime`, `relatime`, NFS-specific options) cannot be
  configured today.
- `StorageClassSpec` is missing `volumeBindingMode` (Immediate vs WaitForFirstConsumer) and
  `allowedTopologies`; topology-aware or late-binding provisioning cannot be expressed.
- `PersistentVolumeSpec` has no `node_affinity` field; topology-constrained CSI volumes cannot
  express which nodes they are accessible from.
- `PersistentVolumeClaimSpec` has no `dataSource` or `dataSourceRef` field; volume cloning and
  snapshot-restore are not expressible.
- The scheduler has no CSI-specific plugins and does not filter nodes by driver availability,
  volume attach limits, or topology constraints.
- If controller and node CSI services are on different sockets, there is no way to configure
  separate controller/node endpoints; a single socket is assumed per driver.

Implementation surfaces
- `tugboat-resources/proto/core/v1/storage_class.proto` — add `controller_create_secret_ref` and `mount_options`
- `tugboat-resources/proto/core/v1/persistent_volume.proto` — add `node_affinity` if topology support is desired
- `tugboat-resources/proto/core/v1/persistent_volume_claim.proto` — add `dataSource`/`dataSourceRef` if snapshot support is desired
- `tugboat-controller-manager/src/provisioning.rs` — resolve `controller_create_secret_ref` and include in CreateVolume/DeleteVolume secrets
- `tugboat-controller-manager/src/pvc_provisioner.rs` — pass provisioner secrets into `CreateVolumeRequest` and propagate `mount_options` into provisioned PV
- `tugboat-controller-manager/src/pv_cleanup.rs` — pass provisioner secrets into `DeleteVolumeRequest`
- `tugboat-csi-operator/src/lib.rs` — thread `mount_flags` through `create_volume` and `controller_expand` APIs
- `tugboat-apiserver/src/endpoints/v1_core/`
- `tugboat-scheduler/src/`

Acceptance criteria
- ✓ PVCs can express the inputs needed for CSI provisioning, including requested size and volume mode.
- ✓ Dynamically provisioned PVs preserve the CSI metadata needed by downstream node-side flows.
- ✓ Drivers that require controller expansion are handled through the controller-side CSI flow.
- ✓ `pv_cleanup.rs` and `pvc_provisioner.rs` handle non-CSI PVs gracefully.
- ✓ All node-side and controller-publish secrets are propagated from StorageClass into the provisioned PV.
- Drivers that require provisioner secrets (CreateVolume / DeleteVolume) can be used without
  embedding credentials in StorageClass.parameters; `controller_create_secret_ref` resolves them
  as a first-class field.
- Filesystem volumes can specify mount options via StorageClass so that `MountVolume.mount_flags`
  is populated and non-default mount behaviour (e.g. noatime, NFS options) is supported.
- The todo stays aligned with reality: it must not describe the above two items as resolved until
  both the proto additions and the controller/operator wiring are in place.
